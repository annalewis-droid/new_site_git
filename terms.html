Written estimates are valid for 15 days from issue date. Work begins only after written approval of the estimate. Any added work, scope changes, material upgrades, or discovered conditions require a written change order before proceeding.

Pricing & payment

The Prime Handyman Service Call is $139 and includes mobilization and the first hour of labor. Additional labor is billed at $99/hour in 15-minute increments. Materials are billed at cost. Jobs with materials over $200 or totals over $400 require a 50% non-refundable deposit unless a manager override applies. Time-window multipliers may apply for after-hours and weekend work as described on the Pricing page.
