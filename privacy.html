Privacy Policy

Prime Handyman Service LLC (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

When you contact us, request an estimate, or schedule service, we may collect:

  • Contact details (name, phone, email, service address)
  • Property details (property type, access notes, pets, hazards)
  • Photos you send of the repair area
  • Job notes, estimates, change orders, and invoices
  • Payment information (processed by a third-party processor; we do not store full card numbers)

How we use it

We use this information to:

  • Prepare estimates, schedule work, and complete repairs
  • Document the work for your records and ours
  • Send invoices, receipts, and follow-up messages
  • Improve our service and respond to questions

How we share it

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who help us run the business (e.g. payment processors, scheduling tools)
  • Licensed trade partners when a job requires specialty licensing
  • Authorities when required by law

Cookies & analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand site traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to update, export, or delete your information at any time by emailing bryan.lewis@prime-handyman-az.com.
