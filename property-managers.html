How It Works for Property Managers

The PM flow, from punch list to walkthrough.

1

Send the Punch List

Email or text a list of repairs per unit. Include property address, access info, and tenant coordination notes.

2

Photo Review & Scope

We review photos, ask clarifying questions, and identify items that may need coordination with specialty trades.

3

Batch Estimate

We send a clear batch estimate per unit with PHSC, labor, materials, exclusions, and any change-order risks identified.

4

Approval & Schedule

You reply "Approved as quoted." We schedule the work, coordinate with tenants, and send an arrival window.

5

Documented Execution

We execute the work with photo documentation. Change orders, if any, are written and approved before proceeding.

6

Invoice & Service Record

Digital invoice, payment collected, and a service record kept for the property. Photos archived and available on request.
