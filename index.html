The Problem Prime Solves

Home repairs should not feel like a gamble.

Most homeowners don't just need "a handyman." They need someone who answers, explains the price, shows up prepared, protects the home, documents the work, and finishes clean.

No-shows and poor communication

Prime sends clear scheduling messages and arrival communication — so you always know when we're coming and what to expect.

Vague pricing and scope creep

Prime uses written estimates, explicit inclusions/exclusions, and written change orders. No surprise add-ons without approval.

Messy or unfinished work

Prime uses before/after photos, cleanup standards, walkthroughs, and invoice closeout — every job, every time.

How It Works

Send the details. Get a clear plan. Approve the scope. We handle the fix.

A documented repair process that respects your time, your home, and your trust.

1

Send the Details

Call, text, or submit the form. Include photos, service ZIP, timing, access notes, pets, HOA restrictions, and any safety concerns.

2

Receive a Clear Estimate

We define the scope, labor, materials, timing, exclusions, and approval method before any work begins.

3

Approve and Schedule

Common Questions

Before you call — the quick answers.

What does the $139 Prime Handyman Service Call include?
It includes mobilization and the first hour of labor. Additional labor is billed at $99/hour in 15-minute increments. Materials, transport, deposits, and approved change orders may apply.
Do you provide estimates?
Yes. We provide clear digital estimates with written scope, pricing, inclusions, exclusions, and approval instructions. Most estimates are based on photos you text us and a brief description.
Can I text photos?
Yes — that's the fastest way to get on the schedule. Text 2–3 photos, your ZIP code, and a short description of the issue to 623-227-8884.
What if more work is discovered?
We pause and document the condition. Added work, upgraded materials, or changed scope requires written approval before proceeding. No surprise add-ons.
Do I need to be home?
An authorized adult should be present unless written access arrangements are approved in advance. We'll coordinate timing with you directly.
Do you clean up?
Yes. Prime protects the work area, controls debris, and performs basic cleanup of the active work area. A walkthrough confirms everything before payment.
Do you offer bilingual service?
Yes — friendly English / Spanish intake is available for clear communication from the first call.
Do you work with landlords and property managers?
Yes. Prime supports punch lists, turnovers, repair documentation, and recurring property maintenance. See our Property Managers page.
