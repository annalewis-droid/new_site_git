Time Windows

Regular, after-hours, and weekend windows.

Most jobs fall into regular hours. After-hours and weekend jobs carry a multiplier that's clearly stated up front.

Time Window Schedule Multiplier
Regular Hours Monday – Saturday, 7 AM – 7 PM 1.0x (base rate)
After-Hours Monday – Friday, 8 PM – 6 AM 0.75x discount
Weekend Saturday 7 PM – Monday 6 AM 1.25x
Heads up: After-hours and weekend multipliers are stated on the estimate and applied only after written approval. They are never applied retroactively.
Deposits & Payment

Clear deposit rules. Multiple payment options.

When is a deposit required?

Jobs with materials over $200 or totals over $400 require a 50% non-refundable deposit unless a manager override applies.

The deposit covers material procurement and locks your job on the schedule. It's applied to the final invoice.

  • Stated clearly in the estimate
  • 50% non-refundable once materials are procured
  • Manager override available for qualified clients

Payment methods

We collect payment at the walkthrough unless other arrangements are approved in writing.

  • Major credit & debit cards
  • Digital payments (Zelle, Venmo, Cash App)
  • Check (with prior approval)
  • Digital receipt emailed after payment
Transport fee: When material pickup or delivery is required, a transport fee applies. The amount is itemized on the estimate and never bundled in.
