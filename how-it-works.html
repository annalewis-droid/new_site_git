The Process

How Prime Works

A documented repair process that respects your time, your home, and your trust. Here is exactly what happens from your first text to the final walkthrough.

1

Lead Intake

You reach out by call, text, or web form. We listen, ask a few questions, and get a feel for scope, urgency, and timing.

2

Photo Review

Send 2–3 photos plus your ZIP code. Photos help us scope faster, reduce surprises, and prepare the right tools and materials for the visit.

3

Written Estimate

We send a clear digital estimate with scope, labor, materials, timing, inclusions, exclusions, and approval instructions. Estimates are valid for 15 days.

4

Approval

You reply "Approved as quoted" by text/email or approve through the website form. This authorizes us to schedule and procure materials.

5

Scheduling

We confirm an arrival window. For larger jobs, we collect any required deposit before locking the date on the calendar.

6

Arrival Communication

We send an arrival text so you know when we're heading over. No guessing, no waiting around all day.

7

Work Execution

We protect the work area, confirm the scope on arrival, and complete the repair with photos documenting site conditions and progress.

8

Change Order Control

If hidden damage, added work, or material changes come up, we pause and get written approval before proceeding. No surprise add-ons.

9

Walkthrough & Cleanup

We walk you through the finished work, document the result with after photos, perform basic cleanup, and confirm you're satisfied.

10

Invoice, Receipt & Follow-up

We send a digital invoice, collect payment, and email a digital receipt. We follow up to make sure everything is holding up — and we're here if anything needs touch-up.

No Surprises Promise

If hidden damage, added work, material changes, or safety concerns appear, we pause and get written approval before moving forward. The price you approved is the price you pay — plus only what you've agreed to in writing.
