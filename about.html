About Prime

Built by a Glendale Native to Raise the Standard.

Veteran-owned, locally operated, and built around a documented repair process that respects your time, your home, and your trust.

The Owner

Bryan Lewis — Glendale Native, U.S. Military Veteran, Field Operator.

Prime Handyman Service LLC was built by Bryan Lewis, a Glendale native, U.S. Military Veteran, and experienced field operator with 17+ years in skilled trades and 6+ years in job-site management.

Bryan saw the same problem over and over: homeowners needed small-to-medium repairs completed by someone who communicated clearly, showed up prepared, protected the property, and finished the job professionally. So he built Prime to do exactly that — and to do it with the kind of process and documentation you can actually rely on.

Prime exists to deliver everyday fixes with prime quality — through clear intake, documented estimates, respectful service, clean work areas, and reliable follow-through.

Why Prime Exists

The handyman industry had a process problem.

Prime is the answer Bryan wished he could hire himself: a documented repair process that makes the work safer, clearer, and more respectful — for both sides.

Clear Scope Up Front

Every estimate defines labor, materials, timing, exclusions, and approval method. No vague quotes, no surprise add-ons.

Photo Documentation

Site conditions, progress, and final results are all captured. You have a record, and we have accountability.

Written Change Orders

Anything new, hidden, or upgraded requires written approval before work continues. Always.

Respectful Job-Site Conduct

Surface protection, clean staging, controlled debris, and a final walkthrough before payment. Your home is treated with care.

Bilingual Intake

Friendly English / Spanish intake available — clear communication from the first call.

Veteran Discipline

Military-grade standards, attention to detail, and follow-through show up in everything we do.
